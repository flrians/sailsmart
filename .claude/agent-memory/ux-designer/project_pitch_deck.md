Pitch deck lives at `previews/pitch-deck-investor-2026.html`. 15 slides, fully in English, self-contained HTML with embedded CSS.

**Why:** Florian needed a pro-quality investor pitch deck for pre-seed fundraising (€150k target, June 2026).

**Design system used:** Outfit font (not DM Sans from original), navy-deep (#001932) dark backgrounds for cover/vision slides, ocean blue (#0077BE) accents, DM Mono for code-like labels and slide numbers. Templates: tmpl-a (dark full-bleed), tmpl-b (white content), tmpl-c (mist/data), tmpl-d (split — defined but not used in final).

## Charter Partner Pitch Deck

`previews/sailsmart-charter-pitch.html` — 14 slides, 16:9 aspect ratio, targeting charter companies as test pilots.

**Design language (Bikeplace-inspired):** Soft mint-to-lavender radial gradient backgrounds (each slide shifts subtly), white rounded cards (radius 18px), bold 800-weight Outfit headings in near-black (#0D0D0D), small colored section labels above headings, 4px colored horizontal bar under the heading, lots of whitespace. Brand color: `#1A6B9A`. No dark backgrounds except cover (slide 1) and close (slide 14) which use deep navy.

**Slide order (14 slides):**
1. Cover (dark)
2. Who We Are (founders)
3. Problem — Guest perspective
4. Problem — Charter company perspective
5. Solution (chat mockup + feature callouts)
6. How It Works (3-step flow)
7. Traction / Bavaria C50 live
8. Why Charter Companies Win (4 benefits)
9. Market
10. Business Model (3-tier: pilot free / per-boat / fleet)
11. Competitive (table: paper / WhatsApp / SailSmart)
12. Test Pilot Program (ask + get)
13. Roadmap (Q3 2026 → Q2 2027)
14. CTA / Contact (dark, Florian's email)
