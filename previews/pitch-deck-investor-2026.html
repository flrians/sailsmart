The Solution
SailSmart — Your digital on-board specialist
RAG-powered AI chat on official Bavaria manuals. With citations. Always available. No hallucinations.

AI Chat with Source Citations

Ask a question → answer from the official manual → link to the exact page. Every response traceable to the source document.

Cabin Configuration Viewer

PDF Manual Viewer
