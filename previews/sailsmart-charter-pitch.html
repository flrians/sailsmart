Bavaria C50 — Charter Guest Chat
How do I start the engine if the bow thruster battery is flat?
The bow thruster has its own battery — the main engine is unaffected. Start normally via the ignition panel. See Engine Manual p. 34.
Where is the fire extinguisher?
Two extinguishers: one under the galley sink, one in the starboard cockpit locker. Both are ABC class powder.

Product
Three steps. Zero complexity.
01
We upload your manuals
Send us your boat documentation. We index everything into SailSmart, configured for your exact models.
02
Guests scan a QR code
A sticker in the cockpit. One scan — SailSmart loads in the browser, pre-configured for that exact boat. No app. No login.
03
Any question → instant answer
SailSmart searches the indexed manuals and returns the right answer with the source page referenced. No calls to base. No waiting.
